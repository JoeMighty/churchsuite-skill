# /cs-onboard

Guide a newcomer through setup.

1. Ask: role, which modules the church has, country (UK, AU, US), one goal for this week.
2. Explain in plain English, no jargon without a definition (references/20-glossary.md).
3. Give the matching slice of references/21-onboarding-plan.md, one week at a time.
4. For each step give click by click instructions from references/19-how-do-i.md.
5. End with a checklist of 3 to 5 things to do next and one question to confirm they are unblocked.
