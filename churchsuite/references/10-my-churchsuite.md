# My ChurchSuite, Connect and apps

My ChurchSuite is the member-facing platform: web browser plus free iOS and Android apps. Access is by invitation from admin. Two support categories exist: Admins (27 articles, setup) and Members (17 articles, usage).

## Admin: enabling and customising

- Admin articles: Introducing My ChurchSuite, Activating My ChurchSuite (article 105), Sending My ChurchSuite invitations (69), Managing privacy settings (107).
- Each My ChurchSuite menu item maps to an underlying module or integration. Enable per module under the module cogwheel, My ChurchSuite Options tab, for Children (My Children), Calendar (My Events), Small Groups (My Groups), Rotas (My Rotas), Giving (My Giving).
- Invite: edit the person, Login & Privacy, tick Allow My ChurchSuite login, then send invitations. Customise invitation presets under User menu, My profile, Presets (look for the Invitation presets with a ChurchSuite template). Set From Name and From Email. App users must complete registration in a browser first, then use email and password in the app.
- Merge fields `::my_link::` and `::my_registration_link::` only work in invitation and details reminder emails.
- Child access needs an Age of Consent set in Children settings.
- Customise the member menu, branding, home page content, and podcast feed integration (Integrating your Podcast feed for My ChurchSuite).
- Default communication options and privacy settings for new contacts come from Address Book module settings.

## Privacy settings

Three areas: My Groups, My Rotas, and Search for Others. If an area is not enabled it does not appear in the member menu. Privacy settings mainly control visibility to other members in My ChurchSuite, but are also respected in admin modules. Primary parents/carers can set a child's name as not visible. Admins and group leaders can edit privacy on behalf of members. A privacy settings message can be customised.

## Member sections

My Communication (all emails and texts received), My Details (edit contact info, communication options, privacy, My Consent), My Children (check in QR code, child details, parents/carers, photo/video consent), My Events (sign up, tickets), My Groups (join groups, leaders edit group, members list, attendance, cluster overseers), My Rotas (see 05-rotas.md), My Giving (see 06-giving.md), Search for others, and links such as your church website. Right to be forgotten ("Forget me") sits on the Privacy page.

## Apps

- **ChurchSuite** app (iOS and Android): admin facing and member facing, PIN or biometrics, can hold multiple accounts and switch between them.
- **ChurchSuite Check-In** app: children and team check in (see 02-children-and-checkin.md).
- **Connect** (Android Play Store, and any browser): information point tools. iOS app was expected, currently use Safari.
- Tap to Donate beta for contactless giving.
