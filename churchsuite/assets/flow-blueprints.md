# Flow blueprints

## Newcomer follow up
1. Visited (auto from newcomer form)
2. Welcome email sent within 2 days
3. Phone call within 1 week
4. Invited to a small group or course
5. Settled (then archive the flow entry)

## New member onboarding
1. Interested in joining
2. Membership chat booked
3. Chat done, key date recorded
4. Welcomed publicly
5. Added to relevant tags

## Volunteer onboarding
1. Expressed interest
2. DBS or safeguarding check requested (key date for expiry)
3. Training done
4. Shadowing
5. Added to rota

## Pastoral care
1. Need identified
2. Assigned to a carer
3. Contact made
4. Review in 4 weeks
Keep notes minimal and permissions tight.
