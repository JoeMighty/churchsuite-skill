# /cs-audit

Run an account health review.

1. Walk through assets/account-health-audit.md section by section, asking what they see in their own account.
2. Mark each item OK, Needs work or Not used.
3. Summarise the top 3 risks (usually: too many administrators, unreviewed Pending contacts, missing privacy statement).
4. Give a dated action list with an owner for each item.
