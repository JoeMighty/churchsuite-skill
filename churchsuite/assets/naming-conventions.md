# Naming conventions

Consistent names stop mess later.

- Tags: Area, then purpose. Example: "Pastoral - Hospital visits", "Event - Christmas 2026".
- Smart tags: prefix "Smart - ". Example: "Smart - DBS due 60 days".
- Key dates: noun phrase. "Baptism", "DBS expiry", "First visit".
- Flows: audience and goal. "Newcomer follow up".
- Custom fields: plain labels, no abbreviations.
- Retire, do not reuse: archive tags you no longer need.
- Write the rules down and give them to every user who can create tags.
