# Regional notes

ChurchSuite is built for the UK first. Check which applies before answering.

## United Kingdom
Gift Aid, DBS checks, UK GDPR, Charity Commission or OSCR rules, HMRC claims. Forget me button needs an uploaded privacy statement.

## Australia
No Gift Aid. Use Safe Ministry and Working With Children Checks (WWCC, varies by state). Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles apply. Giving receipts use tax deductible gift rules for DGR status churches and charities. Confirm with an accountant.

## United States
No Gift Aid, and the Forget me option is not shown. Giving receipts and year end statements follow IRS rules (501(c)(3) tax receipts). Background checks are run by your chosen provider. State privacy laws vary. Confirm availability of modules with ChurchSuite support, some features differ outside the UK.

## Everywhere
Do not assume a UK term applies. Ask the country first when it affects the answer.
