# Smart tag recipes

Build in the module's Tags area. Check the exact condition names in your account.

- Active adults on one site: status is Active AND site is X.
- Newcomers last 90 days: key date "First visit" within the last 90 days.
- DBS expiring in 60 days: key date "DBS expiry" within the next 60 days.
- Not in a group: Active AND not a member of any group.
- Missing email: Active AND email is empty (for data cleanup).
- Givers without declaration: giver has no Gift Aid declaration (UK).

Warning: do not delete the key dates, flows, groups or tags a smart tag depends on.
