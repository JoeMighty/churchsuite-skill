# /cs-gdpr

Handle a data protection request. Not legal advice.

1. Identify the request: access, correction, erasure (right to be forgotten), objection.
2. Explain what ChurchSuite does: for Forget me it notifies the data protection contact and adds a Right to be Forgotten key date. Nothing is auto deleted.
3. Steps: verify identity, find the person across modules (Address Book, Children, Giving, Bookings, Network), export for access requests, decide what must be kept (for example Gift Aid records for HMRC), delete or anonymise the rest, record the decision.
4. Remind: respond within the legal time limit for the country (one month under UK and EU GDPR, extendable in some cases).
5. Suggest asking the church's data protection lead or a lawyer for edge cases.
