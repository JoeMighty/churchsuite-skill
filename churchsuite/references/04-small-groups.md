# Small Groups

Create groups, organise with labels and clusters, manage members and roles, let people sign up, record attendance. Landing article: 560. Getting started: 537.

## Structure

- **Groups**: add, custom fields, images (Managing images), map pin position fixes, flyers (Producing small group flyers), online groups, capacity management, visibility (Show in My ChurchSuite, Show in embed).
- **Labels**: categorise groups (for example Bible study, Activate style). Tags also exist for groups.
- **Clusters**: group groups for oversight and pastoral care. Optional: enable in Module Options, Clusters, Edit. Clusters get a section where you add clusters then assign groups. Communication section can target Groups, Clusters or group Tags. Bulk actions let you email or SMS cluster overseers. Cluster overseers see a Clusters tab in My Groups, but only for groups set to Show in My ChurchSuite and only if the option to manage cluster groups in My ChurchSuite is enabled. By default overseers see only Active groups' member lists and recent attendance.
- **Roles**: assign people to group roles such as Leader. Roles control My ChurchSuite abilities, including Attendance Submission, so you can create a dedicated "Attendance submission" role rather than giving leader rights.
- **Members and pending status**: members can be pending and are handled in attendance recording.

## Sign up

- "Activate" style sign up through My ChurchSuite, your website (Embed) and Connect.
- Person detection during sign up matches existing contacts (see Address Book forms).
- Group capacity limits.

## Attendance (article 93)

- Add through the Small Groups module: Attendance section, Add attendance, choose Group and Date. Advanced Search finds history. View/Edit/Delete actions.
- Leaders (or role holders) can add attendance in My ChurchSuite if enabled for their role.
- Automated attendance reminder emails: edit the group and tick Attendance reminders (April 2025). One click submit from the email.
- Visitors: total attendance minus ticked attendees equals visitors.
- Reports: Attendance Averages (spot people slipping away), Attendance Notes, Attendance Summary, Missing Attendance (groups not submitted for N weeks or months), Monthly Engagement (graphs of engagement over time, filter by tags such as Bible study groups).
- Tracking membership: article 381.

## Privacy

Group members control visibility of their details to fellow members under My Groups privacy settings. Group leaders with module access can set granular privacy on behalf of members who cannot use My ChurchSuite.

## Embed

Groups list or map embed. The Small Groups JSON endpoint was redesigned and documented in March 2025, and the embed script updated accordingly (v5.2.5).
