# Volunteer handout: using ChurchSuite

1. Log in to My ChurchSuite with the link in your invitation.
2. Check your details are right. Update anything old.
3. See when you are serving under My Rotas, Next Serving.
4. Cannot make a date? Organise a swap in My Rotas and tell your team leader.
5. Away for a while? Add Unavailability early.
6. Need help? Contact [name] at [email].
Your information is only used as described in our privacy statement.
