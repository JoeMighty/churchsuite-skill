# Privacy statement skeleton

Adapt with your own legal advice. This is a template, not legal advice.

1. Who we are and who to contact (data protection contact).
2. What we collect (contact details, children's details, attendance, giving, pastoral notes).
3. Why we collect it and our lawful basis for each purpose.
4. Who we share it with (ChurchSuite as processor, HMRC for Gift Aid, payment providers).
5. How long we keep it.
6. Your rights: access, correction, erasure, objection, complaint to the regulator.
7. How to make a request.
8. Children's data and parental responsibility.
9. Date last updated.

Upload the final statement in ChurchSuite so the Forget me option shows for members (UK and EU context).
